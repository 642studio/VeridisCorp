The skeleton uses "ValveBiped" bone naming (Valve's standard biped rig, widely
reused across Source-engine-adjacent community models) — a generic rig naming
convention, not a named copyrighted character. No specific franchise character
match found (unlike victor_von_doom.glb, tv_woman.glb, or
the_perfect_steve_rigged.glb, which were flagged and excluded for that reason
in MODELOS-CANDIDATOS-3D.md).

Used here for mesh geometry and skeleton only, in a static bind pose (the asset
has no baked animation clip — gltf.animations is empty). Material/texture
discarded, replaced with the wireframe + translucent-fill treatment used for
every other humanoid figure in this series (see veridiscorp-retro-figura).
